Join Us

We are building an interdisciplinary team of engineers and scientists dedicated to advancing electrochemical energy storage and sustainable energy technologies. Our research bridges fundamental understanding of materials and electrochemical processes with practical solutions for next-generation batteries.

We are committed to developing our members’ scientific, technical, and communication skills for future careers in academia and industry, while fostering a culture of curiosity, collaboration, integrity, creativity, and mutual respect.

We welcome motivated students and researchers from Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Electrochemistry, Energy Engineering, and related fields. Candidates from other disciplines with expertise relevant to our research are also encouraged to apply.

How to apply

Interested candidates should contact the PI directly by email with a CV and a brief description of their research interests and motivation for joining AES Lab. Please use [AES Lab_Applying Position_Your Name] as the email subject line.

Applications are generally reviewed once a month, at the beginning of each month. Applicants are also encouraged to visit the CEI website for current openings and additional information.

Open Positions
Undergraduate Students

Undergraduate students interested in gaining research experience in battery materials, electrochemistry, and energy storage are encouraged to contact the PI.

Research Assistants

Applicants with a BS/BE or MS degree interested in battery materials and electrochemical energy storage are welcome to inquire about research opportunities.
Outstanding candidates may also have opportunities to pursue graduate studies at VinUniversity.

Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome postdoctoral researchers interested in conducting fundamental and applied research in advanced battery materials, electrochemical energy storage, and sustainable energy technologies.
Applicants should also include selected publications and a brief research proposal describing the research they would like to pursue at AES Lab.

Feel free to reach out at giang.na@vinuni.edu.vn
