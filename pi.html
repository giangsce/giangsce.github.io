Principal Investigator

An-Giang Nguyen, Ph.D.

Center for Environmental Intelligence, VinUniversity · Hanoi, Vietnam

I prefer to be called by my first name, Giang. I received my Ph.D. from the Materials Electrochemistry Laboratory, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Chonnam National University, South Korea, in 2023. Before that, I received my Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering from Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

In late 2023, I wrote a research proposal with my supervisor that was granted $1.08M over 5 years by the National Research Foundation of Korea, at a time when the Korean government substantially reduced R&D funding.

I am open to research collaboration in secondary batteries and related fields. Feel free to reach out at giang.na@vinuni.edu.vn.
