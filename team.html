People of the AES Lab

An interdisciplinary group working on battery materials, electrochemistry and sustainable energy.

Principal Investigator

Dr. An-Giang Nguyen (Giang)

Principal Investigator · Center for Environmental Intelligence, VinUniversity

Giang received his Ph.D. from the Materials Electrochemistry Laboratory, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Chonnam National University (South Korea) in 2023, and his Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering from Hanoi University of Science and Technology. He is an Editor of Scientific Reports and has completed 200+ reviews for 50+ journals.

He has led or contributed to 10+ projects with total funding exceeding $1.1M, from NAFOSTED, VINIF and the British Council.
