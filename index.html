Welcome to
Advanced Energy Storage Lab !

Advanced Energy Storage Laboratory (AES Lab), within the Center for Environmental Intelligence at VinUniversity, was established to conduct cutting-edge research and technology development in electrochemical energy storage systems, advanced battery materials, battery recycling, and sustainable energy technologies.

AES Lab is dedicated to advancing next-generation energy storage solutions that support renewable energy integration, electric mobility, circular economy initiatives, and the global transition toward a low-carbon and sustainable future.

We welcome research collaboration opportunities in secondary batteries, electrochemical energy storage, and related fields.

Feel free to reach out at giang.na@vinuni.edu.vn
